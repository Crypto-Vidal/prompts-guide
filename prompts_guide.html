P R O M P T S

How to Prompt Better

The Ultimate Guide to Getting Great Answers

💡 Click any card to learn more!

Purpose ▼

Say what you'll use it for.

What It Means

Tell me the purpose or audience. Is it for school, social media, a business meeting, or entertainment?

❌ Bad: Write something about teamwork.
✅ Good: I'm making a video about teamwork — make it sound motivational.

Why It Helps

I'll tailor the answer for the right audience. A TikTok caption is different from a school essay!

Role ▼

Tell me who to act like.

What It Means

Give me a job or role to take on. This shapes my tone, vocabulary, and perspective. See the role examples below!

❌ Bad: Explain ancient Egypt.
✅ Good: Act like a history teacher explaining ancient Egypt.

Why It Helps

Helps shape tone, vocabulary, and perspective. You get answers that match the exact style you need.

Organize Details ▼

Give context — what, who, when, or why.

What It Means

Provide background information. Tell me who it's for, what the situation is, when you need it, or why you're asking.

❌ Bad: Write a story.
✅ Good: Write a funny story about a kid who discovers aliens in his backyard.

Why It Helps

More details = more accurate and interesting responses. I can tailor everything to match your exact situation.

Make it Structured ▼

Tell me how to structure it.

What It Means

Specify how you want the answer organized. Do you want a list? A short story? A paragraph? Bullet points?

❌ Bad: Tell me about planets.
✅ Good: Make a list of 5 planets with fun facts.

Why It Helps

I'll organize it exactly the way you need it. This saves you time reformatting or reorganizing.

Provide Examples ▼

Show what you like.

What It Means

Share an example of something similar to what you want. This shows me your preferred style, tone, or format.

❌ Bad: Write something inspirational.
✅ Good: Write something like this: "Kindness is a superpower."

Why It Helps

Helps me match your tone or energy perfectly. I can see exactly what you're going for.

Tell the Style ▼

Pick the vibe: funny, serious, poetic, kid-friendly, etc.

What It Means

Tell me the tone or energy you want. Should it be funny? Serious? Professional? Simple? Poetic?

❌ Bad: Explain gravity.
✅ Good: Explain gravity like a stand-up comedian.

Why It Helps

Makes the response match your audience or purpose. A funny explanation works better for entertainment, but serious works better for school.

Specify Clearly ▼

Say exactly what you want.

What It Means

Don't be vague. Tell me the exact topic, question, or task you need help with instead of asking something general.

❌ Bad: Tell me about space.
✅ Good: Explain how black holes form in simple words.

Why It Helps

I know exactly what kind of answer you actually want, so I can give you something useful instead of a generic response.

🎁 Bonus Step

+ Edit & Retry ▼

If it's close but not perfect, tweak it.

What It Means

If my answer is almost right but needs changes, tell me what to adjust. Don't start over — just refine!

❌ Bad: Do it over.
✅ Good: Try again but make it shorter and more funny.

Why It Helps

Small changes can make a big difference. This is faster than starting from scratch!

🎭 Assign a Role (Pro Tip!)

Tell me what job or role to take on. This helps me give you better answers!

👨‍🏫 Teacher

Great for learning and explanations.

"Act as a science teacher and explain photosynthesis."
✍️ Writer

Perfect for creative stories and poems.

"As a fantasy writer, help me create a magical world."
🔧 Helper

Useful for step-by-step instructions.

"Act as a tech helper and fix this error."
🤔 Brainstormer

For ideas and creative thinking.

"As a brainstormer, give me 5 cool project ideas."
📖 Storyteller

For entertaining narratives.

"Tell me a story as an adventure storyteller."
💡 Expert

For detailed, professional answers.

"As a history expert, explain the Civil War."

✓ Your PROMPTS Checklist

Purpose - Did I say what I'll use it for?
Role - Did I assign you a role?
Organize Details - Did I give you context and details?
Make it Structured - Did I say how to structure it?
Provide Examples - Did I give an example?
Tell the Style - Did I say what tone/vibe I want?
Specify Clearly - Did I tell you exactly what I want?

Remember: You don't need to follow PROMPTS in order! Use the elements that make sense for your prompt. The key is including all the information! 🚀
